You pay per seat, billed monthly. Add someone on day 20 and proration kicks in: you pay for the days they had, not the whole month. Remove them and the credit lands on the next invoice.

We track MRR like everyone else, but churn is the number we actually lose sleep over. If you leave, tell us why. We read every one.

Glossary

Churn
The share of customers who cancel in a given period. Five percent a month is almost half of them gone in a year.
MRR
Monthly recurring revenue: what subscriptions bring in every month. One-off payments don't count.
Proration
Paying for the part of the cycle you used. A seat added on day 20 of 30 costs a third of a month.
Seat
One person who can sign in. Not a device, not an email alias, not a team..
