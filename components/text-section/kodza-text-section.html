02 · Approach

Why we stopped sending pitch decks.

A deck is a promise with nice margins. A working page is the thing itself, so that is what we send now.

The first proof goes out on day three. It is a real address, on a real phone, with your real text in it. If the headline does not fit, you see it. Nobody has to imagine it from a slide.

What you get instead

  • A link you can open on the bus.
  • Your copy in place, with what we cut marked in the margin.
  • One question from us. Not a questionnaire.

It takes us about as long as the deck did. The difference is that nothing gets thrown away when you say yes.
