03 · The catalogue

No product tour. Just where to look.

One screen of the catalogue, and the four things worth knowing on it. Point at one, the rest steps back.

  1. Search

    Type the thing you need: button, menu, hero. Plain names, so the first guess usually lands.

  2. Two shelves

    Interface for what you click, Effects for what moves. Two shelves, no maze.

  3. Filters

    One family at a time. The counts say how many before you click.

  4. The cards

    Each one is a short loop, filmed from the code you copy. No mockup, no render.

02 · The terrace

Table for two. Facing the harbour.

Book online and this is the table you get, on the quay in Le Havre. Here is what comes with it.

  1. Table 12

    On the quay, out of the wind. The one we keep for online bookings.

  2. The view

    The marina, from lunch until the lights come on. Free, like the gulls.

  3. The wine

    Muscadet, poured when you sit down. Not before, and never warm.

  4. The bollard

    Cast iron, older than the restaurant. Not a seat, whatever Instagram says.
