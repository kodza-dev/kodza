Why teams switch

Three reasons to stop babysitting your deploys.

  1. 01

    One command

    quay ship builds, tests and deploys. No YAML copied from a 2019 blog post.

  2. 02

    Rollbacks that roll back

    Every release keeps its build. Going back takes four seconds, not a hotfix at midnight.

  3. 03

    Logs a human can read

    The line that broke is the first one you see. Not line 4,812 of a stack trace.
