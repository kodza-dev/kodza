04 · Product tour

The whole tool, one screen at a time.

No onboarding call, no 14-slide deck. Each screen does one job, and it waits for you to finish reading.

Drag the work onto the days you actually have. It counts the hours for you, and says no before your calendar does.

Every pull request, design and doc that waits on you, in one list. Sorted by who is blocked, not by who shouted.

One command, the checks you already wrote, a link at the end. No dashboard to babysit while it builds.

When something fails you get the line, the commit and the person who can fix it. Not a graph that turned red.

05 · The course

Learn it in the order you will use it.

Four modules, each one a page you will actually build. No theory chapter you were going to skip anyway.

Grids that hold when the copy runs twice as long as the mockup. You build the pricing page first, because that is the one that breaks.

Transitions that say where things went. Durations you can defend in a review, and the reduced-motion path written first, not last.

Fields that keep what was typed, errors that say what to type instead, and a submit button that still works when the script does not.

Minify, cache, measure. Then the boring checklist that keeps the site up while you sleep, and tells you when it did not.
