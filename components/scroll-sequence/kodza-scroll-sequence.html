Le Mur — Étretat, 8:10

One wall. One hole.

Poured on the cliff path, facing inland. Nobody moves in this film, and neither does the camera. Only the sun does.

Noon — sun 38° up

The patch crosses the slab.

The sun climbs and the light through the arch slides across the ground. A sundial nobody asked for.

Evening — sun 14° up

Then the wall takes it back.

Low sun, long shadow. By seven the opening is just a door again, and the scroll gives you the morning back.
