The dough sleeps.

Twelve hours at four degrees and nobody touches it. Flavour is just time you did not rush.

04:00

Hands, flour, no playlist.

The bench is warm and the dough wakes up. Shape it before it makes plans of its own.

05:10

Stone, steam, no mercy.

A wood-fired oven, lit at midnight. Twenty-five minutes, one cloud of steam, and a crust that crackles as it cools.

05:35

Done is a number.

Not “golden brown”, not hollow when you knock. A probe in the middle says 96 and the debate is over.

07:00

Still warm. Yours now.

The shop opens at seven. Ninety minutes out of the oven, the loaf is the temperature of the hand holding it.
