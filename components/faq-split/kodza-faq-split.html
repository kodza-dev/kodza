FAQ · 12 questions

Before you book.

Twelve rooms in a 1950s concrete block, five minutes from the beach in Le Havre. The questions everyone asks, answered once.

Still stuck? Write us. Someone at the desk answers, usually within the hour.

Booking

Do I need an account to book?

No. A name, an email and a card to hold the room. That's the whole form.

Can I change my dates?

Yes, up to 48 hours before you arrive, from the link in your confirmation email. After that, write to us and we'll see what's free.

What if I cancel?

It's free up to 48 hours before you arrive. Later than that, we charge the first night.

Payment

When do I pay?

When you leave, by card, phone or cash. The card you book with only holds the room.

Is the city tax included?

No. Le Havre charges €1.10 per adult per night, added to the bill when you leave.

On site

When can I check in?

From 3 pm. Here early? Leave your bags at the desk and go and see the sea.

Is there parking?

Twelve spaces in the courtyard, €12 a night. Book one with the room: they go first.

Is breakfast included?

No, it's €14, from 7 to 10:30 in the lobby. The bread comes from the bakery across the street.

Is the Wi-Fi any good?

1 Gbit fibre in every room. Fast enough to deploy on a Friday. We still wouldn't.

Leaving

When do I check out?

By 11 am. Ask the night before and you can keep the room until 1 pm, if no one is waiting for it.

Can you keep my bags?

Until 8 pm, in a locked room behind the desk. It's free.

Can I get an invoice for my company?

Yes. Give us the company name and VAT number before you leave, and the invoice comes by email.
