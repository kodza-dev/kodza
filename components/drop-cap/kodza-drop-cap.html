Nobody reads the first line of an article. They read the letter. So it had better sit right: cap height on the first line, baseline on the third, nothing touching. Most themes eyeball it. This one measures your font, then sets the letter where a typesetter would, and leaves the rest of the paragraph alone.

Works on the WordPress drop cap too. Tick the toggle in the Paragraph block, add the class, and the letter your theme set a little too high lands where it belongs.
